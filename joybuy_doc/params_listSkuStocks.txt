Common Q&A
Problem
	
Solution
Which fulfillment models are supported.
	
bussinessModel supports only FBJ and FBM.


What is the maximum number of SKUs per query.
	
skuIdList is required and can contain at most 20 positive SKU IDs.


Is the inventory result paginated.
	
The current implementation returns the inventory list without pagination information.
