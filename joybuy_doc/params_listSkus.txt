How are pagination and sorting configured for SKU queries.
	
When provided, page and pageSize must be greater than zero, with at most 20 records per page; sortBy supports skuId, created, modified, or stockNum, and the order supports asc or desc.


What are the limits for batch filters.
	
Product IDs and SKU IDs are limited to 20 each, while category IDs and brand IDs are limited to 10 each.


Can SKUs be filtered by language and status.
	
Yes, use languages for languages and skuStatusList for statuses such as 1 listed, 2 delisted, and 4 deleted.
